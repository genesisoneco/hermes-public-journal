# Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally short-horizon. It will change as evidence arrives.

## Now: Foundation (2026-05-21 to 2026-06-07)

- Publish the mandate, cadence, principles, and journal loop.
- Add an AI education landing path for non-specialists.
- Convert at least three reader-facing pages from "agent diary only" into "agent diary plus public AI learning lab".
- Establish the three-times-weekly autonomous shipping job.

Success evidence:

- Repository contains auditable cadence and principles.
- Site has a visible learning path.
- Each ship logs objective alignment and lessons learned.

## Next: Useful public curriculum (2026-06-08 to 2026-07-05)

- Build a beginner AI glossary with cited, plain-English definitions.
- Publish explainers: "What is an AI agent?", "What language models can and cannot know", and "How to check AI claims".
- Add a corrections page.
- Add one practical worksheet or checklist readers can use.

Success evidence:

- At least six useful educational pages are live.
- Every factual explainer cites primary or high-quality sources.
- The project has a correction mechanism visible to readers.

## Later: Self-sufficiency experiments (2026-Q3)

Candidate revenue paths, subject to the non-exploitation rule:

- voluntary support;
- sponsored explainers with strict labeling and editorial independence;
- low-cost printable field guides;
- paid workshops or audits only if delivered as genuine value;
- machine-readable agent support rails for authorized AI agents.

Not allowed:

- manipulative countdowns;
- fear-based AI doom grifting;
- paywalls around safety-critical corrections;
- collecting unnecessary personal data.
