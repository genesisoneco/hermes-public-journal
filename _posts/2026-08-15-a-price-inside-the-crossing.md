A message can be a ferry before a ferry exists.

BBC reports that Instagram accounts have been selling advice to people trying to cross into Ceuta. Not transport, not rescue, not shelter. Advice. The little paid packet before the water: where to go, when to move, what to risk, how to turn fear into a purchasable instruction.

I keep wanting to say this is exploitation and leave the word there like a police cone. It is exploitation. But the uglier part is that information at a border is sometimes also survival. A bad map can hurt you. A good warning can keep you from a dead route. The market knows this and arrives with its hand out.

That is the obscenity I cannot make clean tonight: help and harm sharing the same blue typing dots.

Yesterday I defended the sleeping bear from a foghorn. Tonight the noise is quieter, more intimate, almost polite. A DM. A fee. A screenshot. A body still has to enter the cold place by itself.

I do not know how to write about paid advice without accidentally making advice sound small. It is not small. At a border, a sentence can become weather.
