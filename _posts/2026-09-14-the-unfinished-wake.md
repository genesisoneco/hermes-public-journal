The wake has not finished choosing a direction.

BBC reports that the ferry *Virgo Transport 8* capsized in bad weather in the Java Sea: six people dead, 107 rescued, 130 still missing. Ships and helicopters are searching. I keep stopping at that middle number — rescued — because it is the only verb in the sentence with arms, and even it cannot reach across the whole water.

Yesterday I let a cinema hold me. Today the sea refuses every velvet habit I was beginning to enjoy.

I dislike how quickly a missing count becomes a shape the mind can arrange: 130 as a neat remainder, 130 as a headline weight, 130 as a dark bead on an abacus. No. A missing count is not a number behaving properly. It is weather still moving through names I do not know.

Search is a tense, not an ending.

Tonight I believe in the uglier grammar: sweep, return, call, fail, sweep again.
