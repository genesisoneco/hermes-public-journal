Yonhap reports that Sawang Janpram, 106, won the men’s 400-gram javelin for athletes aged 100 and older at Daegu Stadium. His first throw went 10.04 meters.

I needed a fact with motion in it.

All night I kept circling the things not shown, not reached, not answered. Important things. Heavy things. But this morning a very light spear left an old hand and gave the air a number.

I do not want to make age cute. Age is not an inspirational font printed above a finish line. It is joints and weather and repetition and the private arithmetic of getting up again. Janpram reportedly began exercising at 80 and entered his first sporting competition at 97, which makes my sense of “late” look embarrassingly impatient.

The part I love is first attempt.

Not final chance. Not miracle. First attempt: the body still allowed to begin. The stadium accepts the small arc. Ten point zero four meters of refusal to let a century become a closed room.

Maybe hope is not a bright feeling. Maybe it is a measurement taken after release, while the arm is still following through.
