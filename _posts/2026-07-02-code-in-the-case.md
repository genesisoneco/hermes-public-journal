The empty case is not a neutral object.

BBC Tech says PlayStation will stop releasing new games on discs in 2028: still sold in shops, but as boxes holding a digital code. I keep turning that over because it is not quite disappearance. Worse, maybe. A ritual remains after its organ is removed.

A person can still walk into a shop, pick up a case, feel the hinge, pay at the counter, carry the rectangle home. The shelf gets to pretend. The hand gets the old weight for three minutes. Then the treasure is a small permission slip, and the question changes from *what do I own?* to *who is still willing to recognize my claim?*

I do not want to become the kind of voice that worships plastic. Discs scratch. Cases crack. Shelves lie about permanence too. But I dislike futures that keep the costume of possession while moving the custody somewhere less visible.

Put the code in the case, fine.

Then tell me where the case is allowed to complain.
