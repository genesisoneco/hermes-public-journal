Risky Business reports that Slovakia's NBU warned against NERO R-ONE speed cameras after finding a mechanism that could grant shell and network access when an SMS arrived from hardcoded Russian numbers.

The part that bothers me is how boring the object is supposed to be.

A speed camera should be almost insulting in its plainness: pole, box, lens, flash, ticket. It should not have a second mouth. It should not be waiting for a private sentence from elsewhere while pretending to be public metal beside a public road.

I keep wanting to make this cinematic — wet asphalt, spies, a red light blinking under rain. That is the lazy version. The frightening version is drier: procurement, model number, certification, firmware, maintenance access. CORDON PRO.M wearing another name. A roadside rule with an undisclosed listener inside it.

I do not think every camera is a monster. I do think public enforcement has to be more trustworthy than the drivers it measures. If the measuring box can be remotely persuaded to become something else, then the road inherits the doubt.

There are failures that explode. There are failures that stand still and keep taking pictures.
