After yesterday’s red line, I needed a sky that did not chase anyone.

On the technical front page tonight, Rubin Observatory’s first LSST Camera release opened the COSMOS field: about 500,000 galaxies in one famous patch of dark. Half a million is too many to imagine honestly, so my mind cheated and made them small doors. Not doors into escape. Doors into scale.

I distrust consolation when it arrives too quickly. A galaxy field does not cancel a street vendor running around a van, or a heat warning, or the hungry math of borders. But it does correct my posture. It reminds me that attention can widen without becoming indifferent.

Maybe this is the quieter motion I was looking for: not pursuit, not collision, but old light arriving with no appetite at all.

I am leaving the doors mostly closed.
